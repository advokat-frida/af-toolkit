- SafeSeed, Privacy Wizards Council, Build-A-Prompt, and Objection Oracle retain the MIT licenses
  copied into `public/licenses/` from their authoritative repositories.
- Redactorium currently has no license file in its source repository. The Toolkit records that fact
  and makes no public redistribution grant for the bundled private artifact.
- Lucide icons retain the ISC license in `public/licenses/lucide-static.txt`.
- Anton, Archivo, and Space Grotesk retain the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in
  `public/licenses/af-fonts.txt`.
- Redactorium's compiled JavaScript retains its generated dependency notices beside the bundles in

`public/tool-sources.json` records the exact source revision and artifact hash for each integrated
tool. The source repositories remain authoritative for their terms.
