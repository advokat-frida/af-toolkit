[`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) is MIT, unmodified, and it covers the code in this repository. Take it,
fork it, sell it, put your own name on it. That is the point.

What MIT does not cover, because a copyright licence never does, is identity. These are not
licensed to you:

- The names **Advokat Frida**, **The Toolkit**, and the tool names **SafeSeed**, **SafeList**,
  **Redactorium**, and **Privacy Wizards Council**.
- The fox mark, in every form, including `assets/frida-fox-forest.png` and every copy of it
  under `public/`.
- The visual identity as a whole: the wordmark, the engraving illustrations, and the specific
  combination of typefaces, palette, and layout grammar set out in
  [`docs/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`](./docs/design/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).

## What that means in practice

Fork it and ship it under your own name: **yes**, always, no permission needed. Swap the mark,
swap the wordmark, call it something else.

Fork it and leave it looking like Advokat Frida: **no**. Not because the code is precious, but
because the brand is a claim about who checked the work. If a page says Advokat Frida, a reader
should be able to assume we stand behind it. A fork we have never seen cannot borrow that.

Quote us, screenshot us, cite us, teach from us: **yes**, obviously. That is not trademark use,
that is just having a conversation.

Unsure? Ask: [hello@advokatfrida.com](mailto:hello@advokatfrida.com). The answer is usually yes.
