Build-A-Prompt

Because “Fox Blocks” was one typo away from a trip to HR.

A prompt builder that keeps the guardrails on. Pick who the AI should think like, what it’s about, how it should read, and what to do, and it writes a specific, lower-risk prompt. Want to see a subject through two sets of eyes? Add a second perspective and compare. Then copy and paste it into your preferred AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar).
