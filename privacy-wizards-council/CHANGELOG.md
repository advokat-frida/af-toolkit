# Privacy Wizards Council changelog

## 2026-09-22

- Added US state-law applicability and duties paths covering the twenty-state comprehensive-law cohort, including exemptions, sensitive data, minors, opt-outs, rights, assessments and cure/enforcement.
- Added independent state histories, explicit missing-fact outcomes and a combined determination record. Editing one state's answers preserves the others.
- Show all 18 topics by default, with categories and titles ordered alphabetically.
- Keep factual choices in the interview and selected-answer guidance in the outcome and exported record. Changed answers remove obsolete guidance.
- Group next actions into a temporary checklist, with distinct Authority and Next determination panels. Tighten spacing, remove redundant state/progress text and improve wrapping.
- Link ROPA guidance directly to the ICO controller and processor templates and CNIL's template.
- Clarify suspected incidents versus GDPR awareness and remove unsupported indefinite breach-record retention guidance.

Ben completed practitioner review and approved this version on September 22, 2026. The source register retains its existing per-source provenance; this release does not assert a new source-by-source review or enable calendar export.

Validation: 75 tool tests, registry/style/build/artifact checks, the full Toolkit gate, independent source and runtime reviews, and direct desktop/mobile inspection. The detailed evidence is in [the release review packet](../docs/review/pwc-us-state-review.md).
