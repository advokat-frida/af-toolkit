Take it with you

You can just walk off with this. The whole Council is one HTML file — no account, no paywall, no telemetry. Copy the URL out of your address bar, hand it to your own AI, and say “rebuild this for a 40-person shop in three jurisdictions,” or whatever your actual situation is. Steal it. Fork it. Bend it to a situation we never saw coming. A tool that only works as long as you keep coming back isn’t a tool, it’s a subscription.

Not legal advice. These wizards surface the relevant law and walk the analytical structure — edge cases, litigation, regulator-led investigations, and multi-jurisdiction stacks need qualified counsel. A human stays accountable for every determination that leaves the building.
