September 4, 2026 · 0.4.0 four tools

The Objection Oracle retires

  • Objection Oracle removed: its rail item, Home card, route and source are gone. Decide holds the Privacy Wizards Council.
  • The nameplate reads Advokat Frida alone and opens The Dispatch; the first rail item is AF Toolkit, behind a wrench. Back to The Dispatch stays at the foot of the rail.
September 4, 2026 · 0.3.1 one rhythm

The same rules in every tool

  • Six line-heights, one per kind of text, declared at each tool's source; buttons, text actions, inputs and row controls now share one spec each.
  • The Toolkit scales up on a desktop monitor: type, spacing and the working column step together above 1500px, and bounded previews grow with the window.
  • A rendered-style census runs in the gate and fails on any type style, color, or control shape outside the committed baseline.
  • Fixed: a hairline mixed from the wrong ink in three tools, a button that fell back to Arial, two mono font stacks, and a strip of the wrong color under Redactorium's header.
September 2, 2026 · 0.3.0 SafeList

A fifth tool, for the people who send the email

  • SafeList: check a send list against the opt-outs, decide each match with Keep or Remove, and download the checked list with a record.
  • A suppression list older than a day is refused. The record names kept contacts with their reason and fingerprints the removed ones.
  • Manage data now reads in working order: SafeSeed, SafeList, Redactorium.
September 1, 2026 · 0.2.1 canvas fidelity

Every drawn state matches the design

  • Redactorium findings cut to the five evidence columns; the record block is the shared shape.
  • SafeSeed columns read as data rows; every generated row scrolls above the receipt.
  • Privacy Wizards asks one question at a time with Next and Back and a progress line.
  • Build-A-Prompt retired from the Toolkit.
  • Redactorium: signing and the verify page retired; the record is the SHA-256 content hash.
  • Privacy Wizards: every authority links to its official text.
  • The Oracle keeps its 8 and names the owner of the next action.
